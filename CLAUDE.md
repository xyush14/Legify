# Headnote — read this first, every session

**Headnote is an AI junior advocate for Indian litigators — "the smartest junior in the chamber." It is NOT a drafting tool. Never describe, position, build, or reason about it as "a drafter."**

Most users are solo, vernacular, district-and-High-Court advocates who cannot afford or find a good human junior (₹15–30k/mo). Headnote is that junior, in software, in their own language and court format.

## What a junior does — and what Headnote therefore does

Headnote runs the whole chamber workflow, end to end. The four pillars:

- **Remember** — case memory, matter diary, hearing dates, the consultation Recorder (client conversation → work-product report). Headnote remembers every matter.
- **Manage** — Matters / case diary, CNR resolution, daily brief, the matter spine, Document Vault (searchable scanned docs).
- **Research** — real, verified Indian case law (no fabrication, no bluff), "ask this judgment" chat, statute maps (IPC→BNS, CrPC→BNSS).
- **Draft** — bail, discharge, revision, appeal, maintenance, civil suits, replies, etc., in the advocate's own court format (incl. Kruti Dev), mirroring their filed style.

The live product surface (app nav): **Ask · Research · Draft · Matters · Recorder · Documents.**
