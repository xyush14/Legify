## 5) Images (you add these — here's exactly where and what)

The doc reads fine with zero images. But if you want to add them, these three
spots carry the piece. Keep them quiet and human, never stock-photo "AI/tech."

1. **Hero (top, above title):** a real district-court corridor, or a lawyer's desk
   at night, warm lamp, papers. Muted, documentary feel. This is the single image
   that decides whether people start reading.
2. **Mid-piece (right after the pull-quote block):** the new codes — a photo of the
   BNS/BNSS/BSA bare acts, or an advocate's hand on a case file. Optional.
3. **Foot (with the bio):** a small, clean Headnote logo or a plain product
   screenshot (the Section Finder or a Hindi draft). Understated. Not a banner.

**Captions matter for SEO** — write them in plain words that include "advocate,"
"district court," "Hindi," or "BNS" where honest. Send images as separate
attachments; most editors prefer to place them in their own CMS.

**Pull-quote already built into the doc** (designers love a ready one):
> "A junior who has read everything, who does not tire, who does not invent, and
> who will, at last, stay till two."
