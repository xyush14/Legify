Welcome to Headnote

Let's set up your chamber.

Three things, and only the first is required. Give us your name as the court has it and we can pull your pending matters straight off the eCourts record — so you open Headnote to your own cases, not an empty screen.

1
Who you are
This goes on every draft's signature block and vakalatnama, exactly as you type it. It is also how the court record is searched.
Used to narrow the case search to your own courts.
2
Bring in your pending cases
Headnote can read the public eCourts record and file your pending matters into your diary — with their next dates, parties and stage. You tick which ones are actually yours; nothing is saved before that.
Recommended
Tick the matters that are yours. Anything belonging to another advocate with a similar name, leave unticked — you only do this once.
3
Photograph a week of your diary
If your matters are not on the eCourts record — or you keep your own register — photograph a week of pages. Headnote reads each page, you check the rows, and they land on their own dates.
Optional
One photo per day's page. Nothing is filed until you have checked the rows.
Your name and enrolment number are all we need to begin.
