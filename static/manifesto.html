Why Headnote

The courts went digital: e-filing, virtual hearings, records online. The whole system modernised around the advocate. But the advocate stayed exactly where they'd always been. Alone, late at night, chasing a citation through borrowed books, retyping a format from a ten-year-old file. Every profession got its tool. The lawyer never did. So we built it. For the advocate, in their own language, for every court from the district bench to the High Court.
